# Wonder artwork briefs

Three assets were generated separately using the built-in image generation tool. Each is a full 1536 × 1024 illustration, converted to optimized JPEG without cropping. They are artistic settings, not photographs or measured reconstructions. The animated Novi character is rendered separately in 3D. Original generated PNGs were left unchanged.

## merapi

Runtime asset: `public/wonders/merapi.jpg`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production scenic backdrop for NoviVerse, a polished children's Three.js science adventure. Create one landscape image, 1536x1024 or a 3:2 ratio, richly detailed painterly 3D game illustration, natural materials, layered depth, soft cinematic lighting. No text, letters, diagrams, labels, UI, frame, logo, character or spacecraft. Environment only. The scene must look attractive close up. Keep the key discoverable features in the central 30% of image width because phones crop the side scenery. Provide a believable open foreground path/ledge in the bottom quarter for our separately rendered character. Avoid objects cut off at edges except continuous landscape. This is an artistic location illustration, not a photograph or an exact terrain reconstruction. Subject: Mount Merapi on Java seen from a distant safe observation ridge, lush tropical slopes, charcoal volcanic rock textures and pale morning clouds. In central width: the volcano summit and its irregular lava dome near x50% y30%, a deeply eroded flank gully with old gray volcanic deposits near x54% y50%, and a cluster of detailed dark andesite rocks beside the foreground observation path near x46% y70%. A very faint thin vapor plume is okay; do NOT depict a lake of lava, flowing red lava, exploding eruption, visitors at the crater, or a dangerous route. Broad mountain silhouette surrounded by tropical foothills, dramatic yet calm teal dawn and warm light. No active eruption depiction.
